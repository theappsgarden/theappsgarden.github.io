01 / WORKFLOW

From brief to a draft ready for review

We begin with a topic, audience, and available materials. An AI tool helps organise ideas and prepare a first draft. A person then checks facts, tone, structure, and publication.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Define the goal, audience, and reference materials.

  2. 02

    Draft

    Use AI to structure ideas and propose an initial text.

  3. 03

    Review

    Check facts, style, links, and relevance to the business.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Approve the final version and adapt it to the right channels.

Results depend on source quality, instructions, and human review. We do not promise search rankings or a number of leads.
