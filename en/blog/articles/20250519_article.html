EU AI Act: Shaping Global AI Regulation

The EU AI Act is nearing enactment, potentially setting a global benchmark for AI regulation. This landmark legislation aims to promote responsible AI development and deployment. The Act classifies AI systems based on risk, imposing strict requirements on high-risk applications like facial recognition and autonomous vehicles. This could significantly influence how businesses and researchers worldwide approach AI. The act is expected to pass soon, potentially setting a global standard for AI regulation. https://www.datamation.com/artificial-intelligence/weekly-ai-roundup/
