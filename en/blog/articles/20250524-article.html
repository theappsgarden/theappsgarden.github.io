DeWave: AI Model Interprets Brain Activity into Text

Researchers at MIT have unveiled DeWave, an AI model capable of translating a person's brain activity into text using non-invasive EEG data. This groundbreaking technology offers a new communication pathway for individuals with communication difficulties, such as those with paralysis or locked-in syndrome. By decoding brain signals, DeWave holds the potential to restore communication abilities, improve the quality of life for affected individuals, and revolutionize brain-computer interfaces.
