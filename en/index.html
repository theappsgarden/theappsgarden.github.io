The questions we hear most

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We explain everything in plain language and handle the technical side.

What happens to my data?

We sign a non-disclosure agreement and assess each tool’s privacy before recommending it. Work samples must not contain sensitive data.

Will AI replace my team?

Our goal is to free your team from repetitive tasks so they can focus on work that adds value.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. The entire service is delivered remotely through video calls and online questionnaires, wherever you are.

What is taking up too much of your team’s time?

Tell us a little about it. We can arrange a free, no-obligation 30-minute call to see whether we can help.

Please do not include confidential or sensitive information.
