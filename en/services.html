A report to help you decide, not collect options

  • One-page executive summary: what to do, the cost and the estimated gain.
  • Current process map and where time or money is lost.
  • One recommended tool per need, with reasons; at most two when price or your criteria must decide.
  • Monthly cost per tool and total cost.
  • Estimated yearly savings in hours and euros.
  • Risks: GDPR, the EU AI Act and vendor lock-in.
  • A 90-day implementation roadmap.
  • Team training needs.
  • A backup alternative if a tool changes price or disappears.

What’s not included

Implementation, licences and custom development. Implementation is quoted separately based on the report.

What we need from your business

A decision-making contact, available department leads, both questionnaire rounds, current tools and costs, team roles, work volumes and examples without sensitive data.

From kick-off to delivery

  1. Kick-off

    Non-disclosure agreement and a 45-minute call with management to appoint a contact.

  2. Questionnaire · first round

    5–7 days to complete it by department.

  3. Review interview

    60–90 minutes with management and department leads to examine processes.

  4. Questionnaire · second round

    2–3 days to clarify data, share examples and rank priorities.

  5. Analysis

    1–2 weeks to match processes with tools and test candidates.

  6. Delivery and follow-up

    PDF and a 1–2-hour online presentation, two revisions and 30 days of questions.

A questionnaire focused on real work

Eight sections: company and goals, team, current tools, departmental processes, data and documents, customers and communication, constraints and AI experience. We measure tasks, frequency, time, people and errors to estimate savings using your numbers. Allow 45–60 minutes for management and 15–20 minutes per department lead. A four-step method

From the first call to working AI, with clear numbers and no jargon.

  1. 01

    We listen

    We interview your team and look at how you work today: repeated tasks, wasted time and the tools you already pay for.

  2. 02

    We analyse

    We match your needs to our tool catalogue, updated weekly. We test candidates with your real use cases.

  3. 03

    We recommend

    You receive a report explaining what to use, in what order, costs, estimated savings and risks around data, privacy and vendor lock-in.

  4. 04

    We support

    If you choose, we implement it with you. AI Radar alerts you when a better option appears.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We explain everything in plain language and handle the technical side.

How long does the Study take?

2–3 weeks after we receive the completed questionnaire. Information gathering and the interview happen before that.

How much does the Study cost?

€1,500 for up to 50 people, €3,500 for 51–125 and €6,000 for 126 or more. Implementation, licences and custom development are not included.

What if the recommended tool is no longer the best?

AI Radar alerts you. Without it, your report includes alternatives in case things change.

What happens to my data?

We sign a non-disclosure agreement and assess each tool’s privacy before recommending it. Work samples must not contain sensitive data.

Will AI replace my team?

Our goal is to free your team from repetitive tasks so they can focus on work that adds value.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. The entire service is delivered remotely through video calls and online questionnaires, wherever you are.
