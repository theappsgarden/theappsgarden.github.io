THE APPS GARDEN / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We translate what AI can do into processes that make sense for your business.

Do you work with one specific tool?

No. We are independent and select tools based on the process, team, cost, and privacy.

Do you build custom software?

We focus on configuring existing tools. Where useful, we add simple automations and integrations.

What happens after setup?

We train the team, document the workflow, and hand it over so you can continue without relying on us.
