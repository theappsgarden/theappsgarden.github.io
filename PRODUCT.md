# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, managers, and teams looking to implement AI automations or solutions for their companies or departments.

## Product Purpose

The Apps Garden helps organizations identify opportunities to reduce repetitive work, select suitable AI tools, and put them into practice in their day-to-day operations.

## Positioning

An independent AI consultancy that tests tools against real work and recommends what fits the client's needs rather than selling a particular platform.

## Operating Context

The consultancy starts by understanding a client's processes and existing systems, then analyzes opportunities, recommends tools, and supports implementation and training. Services are delivered remotely.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offers an AI Study, guided implementation, ongoing AI Radar monitoring, and training.
- Also presents AI-assisted solutions for content workflows, product videos, and LinkedIn presence, with human review.
- The website is a static site using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Spanish, English, and Catalan page trees.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: The Apps Garden.
- Independent recommendations, grounded in the client's needs and practical testing.
- Clear explanations, privacy awareness, and keeping client teams in control.

## Evidence on Hand

- Service descriptions and current terms: `en/services.html` and localized equivalents.
- Product and workflow examples: `en/products.html` and `en/products/`.
- Company process and positioning: `en/index.html` and localized equivalents.

## Product Principles

- Begin with the organization's actual work, not a tool to sell.
- Test recommendations against real tasks and explain costs, benefits, and risks.
- Support teams in implementing and using solutions, with human decisions and review.
- Communicate clearly and account for privacy when assessing tools.
